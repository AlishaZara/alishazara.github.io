⚽ matchmind - Your Local Brazilian Football Analyst

👋 What is matchmind?

matchmind is a free program for football fans who follow Brazilian football. It brings together the things you usually have to hunt for across many websites: the current Brasileirão Série A table, detailed match statistics, the starting lineups, and a deep archive covering 20 years of Série A history.

What makes it different is the analyst. matchmind includes an AI assistant powered by Gemma, a language model that runs directly on your computer through a tool called Ollama. That means the AI answers your questions about teams, seasons, players and matches without sending anything to the internet and without any kind of account, subscription or login.

You install it once, open it like any other Windows program, and from then on it is a private little football studio that lives on your machine. It is open source, which simply means the code is public and anyone is allowed to read it, improve it, or share it.

🚀 Getting Started on Windows

You do not need to be a programmer to use matchmind. If you can install a normal app and click through a few buttons, you can do this. Set aside around fifteen minutes for the first setup, mostly to let the AI model download in the background.

Step one: visit this link to download the application:

⬇ Get matchmind from GitHub

  1. Open the link on your Windows computer. Copy the address above into your usual browser — Edge, Chrome, Firefox, whatever you already use.
  2. Download the application. On the page that opens, follow the download instructions shown there and save the file somewhere easy to find, such as your Desktop or your Downloads folder.
  3. Keep the file in a normal folder. Create a folder called matchmind in your Documents or on your Desktop and keep everything related to the app inside it. This makes it simple to find later and simple to remove if you ever want to.
  4. Run the application. Double-click the file you saved. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" message, click More info and then Run anyway. This message appears for many small, independently made programs and does not mean something is wrong.
  5. Wait for the window to appear. On the very first launch, matchmind prepares itself. A small window may open in your browser at an address like localhost, which is simply your own computer talking to itself. Leave it open while it finishes.
  6. Let the AI assistant get ready. matchmind uses Ollama to run Gemma on your machine. The first time you open the analyst, it needs to fetch the model file, which is a large download. Keep the app open, stay connected to the internet, and let it finish. Once it is done, everything works offline.
  7. Start exploring. Pick a season, open the table, click on a match, and then ask the analyst a question in plain English or Portuguese, such as "How did the 2019 champions compare to the 2024 leaders?"
One thing to remember: the very first setup is the slowest part. After that, matchmind opens in seconds and the AI works without an internet connection.

🖥️ What your computer needs

The AI runs locally, so it does ask a bit more of your computer than a website would. These are comfortable guidelines rather than strict limits:

Windows 10 or 11A 64-bit PC is required. Most laptops and desktops from the last several years are fine.
8 GB of memoryMinimum. 16 GB or more gives the AI assistant noticeably more room to work.
About 5 GB of free spaceFor the application plus the local Gemma model file.
Internet for setup onlyNeeded for the initial download and model fetch. Afterwards you can work offline.

You will also need Ollama, the free tool that runs the Gemma model on your computer. matchmind is built around it, and the setup instructions on the download page walk you through installing it if you do not have it yet. You do not need to understand how it works — you only need to install it once and leave it running in the background.

✨ What you can do with matchmind

  • Read the Brasileirão table at a glance. Positions, points, wins, draws, losses, goals for and against, and goal difference — all in one clean view.
  • Open any match for the full picture. Score, statistics, and the starting lineups for both sides, laid out the way a broadcast graphic would show them.
  • Ask the local analyst anything. Chat naturally with Gemma and get answers grounded in the football data that ships with the app.
  • Travel back 20 years. Browse two decades of Série A seasons to compare campaigns, champions and club fortunes over time.
  • Follow momentum, not just totals. See how a team has been performing recently and how that compares to its season as a whole.
  • Keep it private. No account, no tracking, no data leaving your machine.

🧠 How the local AI actually works

Most AI assistants you have used live on a company's server: you type a question, it travels across the internet, an answer comes back. matchmind flips that around. The Gemma model is downloaded once to your computer and runs there, using Ollama as the engine underneath.

Practically, that gives you three useful things. First, privacy: your questions and your browsing stay on your own machine. Second, reliability: once the model is in place, the analyst keeps working even if your connection drops. Third, honesty: the assistant answers using the football data bundled with the app rather than whatever it might have picked up elsewhere, so its answers are tied to the tables, stats and lineups you can see on screen.

If you are curious about the technical side, matchmind is written in Go for the application logic and Vue for the interface. You never have to touch either of those to use the app.

📊 Making sense of the tables and stats

The table view is the heart of the app. Each row is a club, and each column tells part of the story: how many matches have been played, how many were won, drawn and lost, how many goals went in and how many were conceded. The goal difference column is often the quickest way to spot which teams are genuinely strong and which are riding their luck.

Clicking through to a match gives you the detail behind the scoreline — the statistics that explain why a result went the way it did, and the lineups that show who was actually on the pitch. Combined with twenty seasons of history, this lets you ask questions no single-season table can answer: is this title race tighter than past ones? Has this club's defence improved or declined over the years? Which season produced the most dramatic finish?

🧰 If something does not work

Almost every first-time problem comes down to one of four things. Work through these in order before doing anything else.

  • The app will not open. Check that you saved the file in a normal folder such as Documents or Desktop, not inside a temporary download location, and that Windows did not silently block it. Right-click the file, open Properties, and look for an Unblock checkbox.
  • The page opens but looks empty. Close the browser tab, make sure matchmind is still running, and open the address again. Refreshing once usually fixes it.
  • The AI says the model is missing. Ollama is either not installed or not running. Install it from the link on the download page, launch it once, and start matchmind again so it can find the model.
  • The AI is very slow. Local models use your processor and memory heavily. Close other large programs, and avoid asking several long questions at once. A machine with 16 GB of memory will feel significantly smoother than one with 8 GB.

If none of that helps, open an issue on the project page with a short description of what you clicked and what happened. That is the fastest way to get a fix.
